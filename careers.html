Applicant Clarity

Questions answered before you apply.

No inflated promises. No confusing process. Every track is governed by eligibility, contribution and review.

Will everyone receive certificates and rewards?

No. Certificates, incentives, LOR support and recognition are issued only after eligible contribution, verification and policy compliance.

Who can apply for the Campus Ambassador track?

Students with communication ability, campus reach, responsible conduct and interest in outreach or leadership activities can apply.

How are trainer applications reviewed?

Trainer profiles are reviewed based on domain skill, delivery ability, communication quality, sample topics and professional reliability.

Can institutions request custom programs?

Yes. Colleges and departments can request workshops, SDPs, FDPs, internships, hackathons and research-support activities based on requirement review.
