What Ambassadors Do

Ambassadors act as responsible student representatives for Scienwave programs and initiatives.

  • Share program information ethically
  • Coordinate student interest forms
  • Support campus awareness activities
  • Refer verified learners to programs
  • Assist online/offline event promotion
  • Maintain professional communication

Benefits & Add-ons

Ambassadors receive career-building assets that can strengthen their profile when earned through performance.

  • Campus Ambassador Certificate
  • Digital badge and role recognition
  • LOR eligibility for top performers
  • Priority internship review
  • LinkedIn endorsement consideration
  • Monthly recognition leaderboard

Policies, Rules & Safety

The program is performance-based and must be handled professionally.

  • No fake registrations or duplicate entries
  • No misleading fee, certificate or placement claims
  • No unauthorized use of Scienwave brand
  • Incentives after verification only
  • Certificates based on actual contribution
  • Misconduct may lead to removal
