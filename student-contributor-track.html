Rules & Quality Standards

Contributors must maintain originality, punctuality and professionalism.

  • No copied or AI-dumped work without review
  • No misuse of internal information
  • Deadlines must be respected
  • Communication must remain professional
  • Certificate only after verified contribution
  • Low-quality or unethical work may be rejected

Selection Process
  • Submit profile and preferred contribution area.
  • Scienwave reviews your interest and availability.
  • A suitable task may be assigned after review.
  • Work is checked for originality, quality and usefulness.
  • Verified contributors may receive recognition and future opportunities.
Recognition System
  • Verified Contributor Certificate for meaningful contribution.
  • Top Contributor recognition for strong output.
  • Coordinator consideration for consistent contributors.
  • Priority review for ambassador, research or event roles.
Important Conditions
  • Application does not guarantee task assignment or certificate.
  • Recognition depends on quality, originality and completion.
  • Scienwave may revise tasks, timelines or program structure.
