Everything a trainer needs to know before applying.

This page explains responsibilities, benefits, incentives, standards and selection rules clearly, so every trainer understands how to work with Scienwave professionally.

What Trainers Do

Trainers deliver practical learning experiences that help students understand concepts, build projects and gain industry-oriented confidence.

  • Conduct workshops, webinars and bootcamps
  • Prepare session flow and practice tasks
  • Guide project reviews and doubt sessions
  • Maintain attendance and delivery reports

Benefits & Add-ons

Approved trainers receive professional visibility and structured growth support through Scienwave programs.

  • Professional trainer certificate
  • Profile visibility for selected sessions
  • LinkedIn recommendation eligibility
  • Event speaker opportunities
  • Research and innovation collaboration
  • Priority for future paid assignments

Incentives & Amounts

Trainer payouts are assignment-based and confirmed before the session. Amounts depend on duration, topic complexity, audience size and delivery responsibility.

Micro Session₹500 – ₹1,500 for approved short sessions or orientation modules.
Workshop₹1,500 – ₹5,000 for approved half-day/full-day workshops.
Bootcamp₹5,000 – ₹15,000+ for multi-session bootcamps, subject to written approval.
BonusTop-rated trainers may receive recognition, repeat assignments and performance bonuses.

Policies, Rules & Quality Standards

Scienwave maintains professional standards for all training delivery. Association is not automatic employment unless separately issued in writing.

  • No false promises to students
  • No plagiarism or copied materials
  • No misconduct, harassment or misuse of brand
  • Payment only after verified completion
  • Certification subject to quality review
  • Scienwave may pause association for violations

Selection Process
  • Submit application with resume and topics.

Recognition System

Important Conditions

  • Certificates and LORs are merit-based and not guaranteed by application alone.
  • Scienwave may update program scope, incentives and selection criteria when needed.
