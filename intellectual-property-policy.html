Scienwave Materials

Website content, text, layouts, graphics, logos, curriculum, training materials, proposals, forms, certificates, posters, templates, code samples, session plans, documents and brand assets belong to Scienwave or its licensors unless stated otherwise.

Permitted Use

Users may view website content and download/share official materials only for personal, academic, internal institutional review or approved collaboration purposes.

Restricted Use

Copying, reselling, rebranding, publishing, modifying, uploading, distributing, training others using Scienwave materials, using the logo, issuing fake certificates or presenting Scienwave content as your own is prohibited without written permission.

Participant Work

Project work submitted by students or participants may remain theirs unless a specific agreement states otherwise. However, by submitting work to Scienwave, participants allow Scienwave to review, evaluate, store and use it for program administration and verification.

Institutional Proposals

Proposals, curriculum outlines, commercial documents and collaboration plans shared with institutions are confidential and must not be forwarded, copied or used with third parties without written permission.

Violation

Unauthorized use may result in removal from programs, cancellation of collaboration, withholding of documents, takedown requests, claims for damages or legal action.

Contact: For policy-related questions, requests or grievances, email office@scienwavetechnologies.in. Scienwave aims to respond within 2–5 business days where practical.
